Our studio

A Northern kind of imagination.

Northern Flights Gaming develops original games and characters within the Northern Flights creative universe.

Our games take that world in different directions: platform adventures, arcade battles, kart racing, maze chasing, and a new spin on classic cards. Familiar faces connect the collection, while each game has its own way to play.

Part of Northern Flights

Northern Flights Gaming operates as a gaming initiative of Northern Flights Disc Golf Inc., Canada. Visit our official nonprofit website to learn about Northern Flights Disc Golf and its activities.
