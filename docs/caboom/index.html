In development

Big blasts. Bigger chaos.

A retro-inspired top-down arcade bomber set in the Northern Flights universe. Choose your character, outwit AI opponents, and turn the arena upside down with bombs, power-ups, and throwable Chaos Discs.

  • 50 rounds of Last Player Standing
  • 25-level Classic Campaign
  • Character selection and AI opponents
  • Bomb upgrades and throwable Chaos Discs
  • Sudden Death arena closures
  • Local high scores and campaign trophies
  • Touch controls for Android and iOS layouts

Where to play

Android testing build; iOS export planned. No public mobile release verified.
