Caboom of Chaos
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Publisher: Northern Flights Disc Golf Inc.
Gaming division: Northern Flights Gaming
Country: Canada

This policy describes the Caboom of Chaos development version 1.0 inspected on November 19, 2026, including its Android release configuration. A public Android or iOS release is not announced by this policy. The final iOS export has not yet been verified.

Information stored on your device

The game saves information locally so it can remember your preferences and progress:

  • Player-entered nicknames, limited to 12 characters, alongside high scores, levels reached, and score dates. The local high-score list keeps up to ten entries.
  • Campaign checkpoints, selected character, difficulty, upgrades, and relevant game progress.
  • Achievements, campaign trophies, championship results, and unlocked music.
  • Audio levels, fullscreen preference, screen shake, reduced motion, and touch-control preferences, including size, opacity, joystick deadzone, and left-handed controls.

These records are stored in the game’s local save file, monster_bomber_save.json. A local backup file may also be kept to recover saved progress. Nicknames can identify you to other people using the same device; use a nickname you are comfortable displaying locally.

Collection, use, and sharing

In the inspected version, this information is used on your device for gameplay, saved progress, local scores, and settings. We found no game code that sends these records to Northern Flights or an external server. There is no account sign-in, cloud save, or online leaderboard in this Caboom version.

No analytics, advertising, tracking, or external service SDKs were identified in the inspected game source, plugin configuration, or Android dependency setup. The inspected game does not sell or share its local save information with third parties.

Device permissions and connectivity

The Android debug and release export presets disable internet access. The inspected merged Android release manifest requests no Android permissions and disables Android app backup. No camera, microphone, location, contacts, or advertising identifier access was identified.

The iOS export preset does not configure sensitive-data permission descriptions or third-party plugins. Because a final exported iOS app was not available for verification, iOS permissions and platform declarations must be checked before an iOS release. This policy will be updated if the released version’s practices change.

Retention, deletion, and control

Saved information remains locally until it is replaced or deleted. You can remove the game’s local data using your device’s app-data controls where supported, or remove its local save and backup files on desktop. Uninstalling generally removes app-local information, but device backups or platform restore behavior can vary. Deletion can permanently remove your scores, trophies, settings, and campaign progress. Northern Flights cannot retrieve local progress from a server.

Children and shared devices

The inspected version does not request an account, age, or contact details and does not transmit local nicknames or scores. On a shared device, other users may see saved nicknames and game progress. Avoid entering a real name or contact information as your nickname.

Support and external websites

If you contact Northern Flights, we receive the information you choose to provide to address your request. Please share only what is necessary. The contact page, app stores, device operating system, and hosting providers operate separately and may process information under their own policies. This policy describes the game’s practices, not those separate services.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if the game’s data practices change, including if online features, SDKs, or new permissions are introduced.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Northern Flights Disc Golf Inc., operating gaming activities as Northern Flights Gaming, through the official Northern Flights contact page. Identify your message as “Caboom of Chaos privacy.”
