Caboom of Chaos support

For bug reports, gameplay problems, mobile controls, app support, or privacy questions, include “Caboom of Chaos” in your message.

  • Bug reports: describe what happened, what you expected, and how to reproduce it.
  • Device details: include the game version, platform, and device model when relevant.
  • Mobile controls: open Settings, then Touch Controls. The inspected development build includes size, opacity, joystick deadzone, and left-handed options.
  • Progress and scores: these are saved locally in the inspected Caboom build. Avoid clearing app data if you want to keep progress.
  • Privacy questions: read the Caboom privacy policy, then contact us with your question.

Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in a bug report. Screenshots are helpful if they do not contain private information.

Other games

Include the game name and whether you are playing the browser version or a testing build. Public availability and support details can differ between games.
