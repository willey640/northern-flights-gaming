Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026
Developer and publisher: Northern Flights Disc Golf Inc.
Gaming division: Northern Flights Gaming
Privacy contact: info@northernflightsdiscgolf.ca

This policy covers the current local-only DiscMon game for Android and iOS, reviewed on November 20, 2026. It also explains visiting this policy page and contacting us. It does not cover older DiscMon browser versions that use an online score service. This policy does not announce an app-store release: no signed Android release is available yet, and a final signed iOS app has not been verified.

Information stored on your device

DiscMon keeps its Top 10 high scores on your device. If you choose to save a qualifying score, it stores your nickname (up to 20 characters), score, highest level reached, save date and time, and a randomly generated identifier for that run. The run identifier prevents duplicate entries; it is not an advertising or persistent device identifier. These records are displayed in the local leaderboard and used to show your highest score.

The game also saves your music and sound-effect volumes and touch sensitivity locally. Active gameplay state is held in memory; this policy does not promise saved or resumable runs. There is no account registration, cloud save, or online leaderboard in this local-only version. Nicknames and scores are not uploaded to Northern Flights or published to other players.

Network connections, analytics, advertising, and sharing

The reviewed gameplay and save code makes no network requests. No analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-report upload, social sign-in, or third-party data-collection SDK integration was found in the current game source and export configuration. The game does not access advertising identifiers or a persistent device identifier. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

We do not receive, sell, rent, or share the locally saved nicknames, scores, run identifiers, or settings. They are used on your device for the features described above. The inspected Android APK has no Internet permission and disables Android app backup. The reviewed iOS configuration disables tracking and declares no tracking domains or off-device data collection.

Retention and deleting your scores

Scores remain on your device until cleared, displaced from the Top 10, or removed with the app's data. There is no timed expiry. The save system keeps two alternating snapshots for recovery, so a previous snapshot may contain an earlier Top 10 even after a score drops off the displayed list.

  1. Open Options and select LEADERBOARD, or open the leaderboard from the main menu.
  2. Select Clear Scores.
  3. Confirm the clear. Cancel leaves your scores unchanged.

A successful clear writes an empty score snapshot and removes or overwrites the previous snapshot. This clears all locally saved nicknames and scores together, while keeping your audio and touch settings. There is no individual-entry deletion control. If the game reports that scores could not be cleared, retry; do not assume deletion succeeded. Clearing scores cannot be undone through the game.

On Android, you can also use the device's Settings > Apps > DiscMon > Storage > Clear storage/data (wording varies). This removes settings as well as scores. On iOS, use Delete App to remove the app and its local data; Offload App preserves data. Backups, device transfers, and restored copies managed by your operating system may retain earlier data. Manage those copies through your platform's controls. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete files stored only on your device.

Security and shared devices

Native saves are kept in the app's local storage and rely on the operating system's app isolation and your device security. Score snapshots include a checksum to detect corruption; the game does not encrypt the save records. A checksum is not a guarantee against tampering or unauthorized access. No storage system is completely secure. Anyone able to use your game installation may see the saved nicknames and scores. Use a nickname that does not reveal your real name, contact details, or other private information.

Opening this policy and other services

Selecting Privacy Policy in Options opens this HTTPS page in your external browser and needs an Internet connection. The game does not attach nicknames, scores, or run identifiers to that link. Browsing the page makes normal web requests to GitHub Pages, which hosts this website. GitHub may process connection data such as your IP address and request information for hosting and security under GitHub's privacy statement. This static policy page adds no analytics, advertising, sign-in, or cookies.

Apple, Google, your operating system, and your browser may process information independently when you use their services, including app distribution, device backups, and any diagnostics you choose to share. Their own privacy policies apply. This is separate from DiscMon uploading your saved game data.

Privacy questions and support

If you email info@northernflightsdiscgolf.ca, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. These communications pass through email service providers and are used to handle your request. Please send only necessary information and never include passwords. Support correspondence is separate from local game saves and may remain in email records or backups; no fixed support-email retention period is stated here. You may use the same address to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us, subject to applicable legal obligations and backup limitations.

Children's privacy

The local-only game does not ask for age or contact information and does not transmit children's nicknames or scores. The same local storage and deletion controls apply to all players. Parents and guardians can clear scores on shared devices and contact us about information sent in a support message.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if DiscMon's data practices change, including the addition of online features or third-party SDKs. Check that the policy applies to the version you use.
