Privacy information

Privacy, game by game.

Each game can have different features, services, and data practices. Read the policy for the game and version you use.

Caboom of Chaos privacy policy

Local saves, nicknames, high scores, achievements, touch settings, and the inspected development version’s permissions.

DiscMon privacy policy

Current local-only Android and iOS builds: nicknames, Top 10 scores, settings, deletion, and privacy contact. Older online browser versions are outside this policy’s scope.

Other game policies

Policies for Chains of Chaos, Carts of Chaos, and Cribbage of Chaos will be linked here as their release-specific information is verified. The Caboom policy does not apply to those games.

This website

This static site does not add analytics, advertising scripts, accounts, cookies, or browser storage. External game, store, and contact links open separate services with their own practices. Hosting providers may process connection information such as IP addresses and server request logs; see GitHub’s privacy statement for GitHub Pages hosting.
